Meet Mary Sherman.

Experienced in the details.
Invested in the people.

With over eight years of experience, my work sits at the intersection of program management, child protection, and effective implementation. I believe strong systems should make it easier for people to do meaningful work.

I recently served as a Program Manager at Global Refuge, leading technical support for four long-term foster-care programs for unaccompanied children. Along the way, I onboarded and launched two new programs and provided training and daily support to teams navigating operational challenges.

Working across federal, state, and private stakeholders to align grants, hiring, and contracts with agency standards.
